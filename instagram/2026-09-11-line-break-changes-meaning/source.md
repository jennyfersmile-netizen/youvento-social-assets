## Slide copy

1. Private frustration: You shortened the copy. Made the type bigger. It still feels strangely hard to read. Title: A line break can change the meaning.
2. A line break is a pause you add. The reader may hear a pause even when the sentence does not need one.
3. Keep meaning-groups together. Awkward: MAKE THE NEXT / STEP EASY TO / SEE. Natural: MAKE THE NEXT STEP / EASY TO SEE.
4. Three checks before styling: read it aloud; find the phrase carrying the idea; keep small connector words with what they connect.
5. The useful test: Cover the design and read only the stacked lines. Does the sentence still sound like something a person would say? Save this before your next export.
