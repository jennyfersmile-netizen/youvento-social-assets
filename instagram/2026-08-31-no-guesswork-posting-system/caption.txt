You can have time set aside to post and still lose most of it deciding where to begin.

A no-guesswork system does not need dozens of categories or a complicated calendar. It needs a short sequence you can repeat: choose the job, decide the message, pick the format that serves it and finish with one sensible next step.

The format can change. The order of decisions stays steady. That is what turns a blank slot into a post with a reason behind it.
