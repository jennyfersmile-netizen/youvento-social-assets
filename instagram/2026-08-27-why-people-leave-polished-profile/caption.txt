You can polish every detail of your profile and still watch a new visitor leave without taking the next step.

The problem is not always the quality of the design. A profile can look considered while asking people to work too hard to understand what you offer, who it is for and where they should begin.

Keep the polish. Bring the clarity forward: say the offer plainly, give one honest reason to trust it and point to one sensible next step. The design then has somewhere to lead.

Save this before your next profile review.
