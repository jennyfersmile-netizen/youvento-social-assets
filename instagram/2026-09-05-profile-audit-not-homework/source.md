## Slide copy

1. You opened the audit. Now everything feels urgent. Your profile audit should not become homework.
2. Do not begin with the longest section. Choose the fix that changes what a stranger understands first.
3. Give every fix a place in time: Now, Next, Later.
4. Turn each recommendation into one clear choice: Change, Keep, or Test.
5. The goal is not to finish the document. Make the next useful change easier to see—and easier to begin.
