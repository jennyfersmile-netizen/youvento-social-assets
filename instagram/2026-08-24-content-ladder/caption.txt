You can post often and still leave people standing at the first rung.

A new visitor is not ready for every message at once. First they need to recognise themselves in the problem. Then understand your offer, see a believable reason to trust it and know what action makes sense.

When every post tries to sell immediately, the middle of that journey disappears. Give each piece of content one rung to move people up.
