## Caption

You can sense that something on your profile is making people hesitate without knowing which part deserves attention first.

That is why we do not begin an audit with colours or templates. We read the page as a stranger would: what becomes clear immediately, where the path becomes uncertain and what evidence makes the next step feel reasonable.

The useful result is not a list of everything that could change. It is a priority order—so you know which fixes will make the profile easier to understand before you touch the rest.
