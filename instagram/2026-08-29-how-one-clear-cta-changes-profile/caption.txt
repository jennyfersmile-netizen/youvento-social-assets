You can make every part of your profile clear and still feel the ending dissolve into “check it out,” “learn more” and “DM me.”

One clear CTA does not make the page pushy. It resolves a choice. Once someone understands the offer and sees a reason to trust it, they should not have to compare three different instructions before deciding what to do.

Choose the action closest to what a new visitor can reasonably do next. Then let the bio, pinned content and captions support that same path instead of opening new ones.

Fix yours: write the primary action in six words or fewer, then remove the competing instructions.
