## Caption

You can love a profile image at full size and still lose it the moment it appears beside a comment.

That tiny circle has a demanding job. It needs to survive the crop, separate from the background and leave one recognisable shape or detail behind. Extra elements that feel thoughtful on the original often turn into visual noise at profile size.

Test the real use—not only the file on your screen. If one answer is no, simplify the crop before replacing the whole identity.
