# Youvento Reel Script

## Plan item

**Title:** Reel: brand glow-up in 15 seconds  
**Format:** Reel script only  
**Register:** Vibrant — pink, violet and blue  
**Approximate duration:** 15 seconds  
**Production:** Silent Reel. No music, voiceover, sound effects, filters, stickers, auto-captions or other platform overlays.

## Creative direction

Show the emotional shift from quietly avoiding your profile to feeling that it finally represents the business. Use a fictional or Youvento-owned demonstration brand only, and label every profile view **EXAMPLE BRAND**. The transformation is a process illustration, not a client result.

Keep the edit clean and editorial. The on-screen text carries the full message. Use the exact canonical Youvento logo on the final frame without redrawing, distorting or changing its spacing.

## Beat-by-beat script

### 0:00–0:02 — Private frustration

**Visual:** A finger pauses above “Share profile,” then pulls away. Cut to an inconsistent example grid.

**Exact on-screen text:**

`Your brand isn’t bad.`  
`It just feels like five versions of you.`

**Editing note:** Begin on the hesitation. Let the second line land after a short pause.

### 0:02–0:05 — Before

**Visual:** Quick close crops of a mismatched bio, unrelated fonts, disconnected colours and unclear covers. Keep **EXAMPLE BRAND** visible.

**Exact on-screen text:**

`BEFORE`  
`Mixed message. Mixed look. Buried next step.`

**Editing note:** Use three clean cuts. Do not add arrows, stickers or decorative overlays.

### 0:05–0:08 — Direction

**Visual:** Three editorial cards lock into place: message, visual direction, next step.

**Exact on-screen text:**

`Keep the truth.`  
`Remove the noise.`  
`Choose one direction.`

**Editing note:** One line per cut. Keep the pace calm enough to read comfortably.

### 0:08–0:12 — System

**Visual:** The same example profile reorganises into a coherent grid with a clearer bio and recognisable covers.

**Exact on-screen text:**

`One message.`  
`One visual language.`  
`One clear next step.`

**Editing note:** Show the same fictional business, not a replacement business.

### 0:12–0:14 — Payoff

**Visual:** Before and after appear side by side. Label both sides **EXAMPLE BRAND**.

**Exact on-screen text:**

`Same business.`  
`Easier to recognise.`

**Editing note:** Hold long enough for the comparison to register. Do not imply measured results.

### 0:14–0:15 — End card

**Visual:** Youvento pink–violet–blue gradient with the exact canonical white logo.

**Exact on-screen text:**

`A glow-up starts with direction.`  
`Link in bio.`

## Filming and editing checklist

- Canvas: 1080×1920, vertical 9:16.
- Duration: approximately 15 seconds.
- Keep text inside the central safe area, clear of Instagram interface controls.
- Use a fictional or Youvento-owned demonstration profile only.
- Keep **EXAMPLE BRAND** visible on every example profile view.
- Use clean cuts and subtle editorial motion only.
- No music, voiceover, sound effects, filters, stickers, auto-captions or extra platform text.
- Use only the exact canonical Youvento logo on the end card and preserve its proportions and spacing.
- Export a real MP4 only after the footage or screen recordings have been produced. Do not use a placeholder.

## Instagram caption

It is the moment every piece stops arguing with the next. The bio, feed, templates and video covers begin to feel like the same business.

This 15-second concept turns that shift into one clean sequence: confusion, direction, system, recognition.
