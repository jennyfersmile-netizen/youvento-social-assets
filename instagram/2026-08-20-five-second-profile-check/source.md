# The five-second profile check

Format: Branded Educate — single 1080×1350 infographic
CTA: Audit yours

## Visual copy

- Who is this for?
- What do you actually do?
- Why should they believe you?
- What should they do next?
- If one answer is missing, that is your first fix.

No music, audio, filters, overlays, stickers or platform edits.
