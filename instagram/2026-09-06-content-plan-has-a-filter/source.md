## Slide copy

1. You keep saving good ideas. The calendar still feels crowded. A good content plan has a filter.
2. A good idea is not automatically the right post.
3. Let every idea answer three things: Relevance, Role, Route.
4. Then give the idea one of three answers: Keep, Reshape, Release.
5. The point is not to have fewer ideas. It is to face fewer decisions when it is finally time to post.
