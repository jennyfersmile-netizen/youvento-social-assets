You can make the headline bigger, brighten the button and bold the important sentence—then wonder why the post feels harder to read.

The problem is not a lack of emphasis. It is that every element is asking to be first. A clear design gives the eye an order: one idea arrives immediately, the supporting point follows and the details wait until they are needed.

Choose the focal point before styling the rest. Scale, contrast, space and position can all create attention, but they work best when they agree on the same destination.
