## Caption

You can spend twenty minutes polishing a bio and still feel unsure when someone new lands on the profile.

Usually, the missing piece is not personality. It is sequence. A stranger needs to recognise that the page is for them, understand what you help with and see one natural next step—before the wording can do any charming.

Read yours once without filling in the gaps from memory. If a new visitor would need context you have not written, that is the line to fix first.
