## Slide copy

1. You want the launch post to feel bold—and worry it will stop sounding like you. Your brand can change tone without changing identity.
2. Tone answers the moment. Identity answers who is speaking.
3. Keep three things steady: what you believe; words you would never use; how people should feel.
4. Let the volume move, not the centre. Education can be patient, promotion confident and celebration bright.
5. Before you post in a new tone, ask: If the colour disappeared, would this still feel like us? Save this before writing in a new tone.
