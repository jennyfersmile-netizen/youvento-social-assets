You can have the right colours, elegant templates and a beautifully tidy grid—and still feel uneasy when someone asks what the profile is really saying.

That feeling usually means the visuals are consistent, but the message is not. Each post looks finished, yet the page does not build one clear impression of who you help, what you offer or why it matters.

The fix is not making the feed less beautiful. It is deciding what each post needs to make clearer before you design it.
