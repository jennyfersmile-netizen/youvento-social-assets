You can feel ready to post and still wonder whether one small mistake is hiding in the design.

The final review is rarely a dramatic redesign. It is a short change of perspective. Shrink the cover until it behaves like a grid thumbnail. Read every slide at the size it will reach someone’s hand. Follow the message from the first question to the final ask, then inspect the edges, logo, contrast and order.

These checks are not about chasing perfection. They are about removing small obstacles before the reader has to meet them.

Save this as your pre-export checklist.
