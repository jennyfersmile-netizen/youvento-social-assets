You can choose colours you genuinely love and still hesitate every time a new post opens in front of you.

The problem may not be the palette. It may be that none of the colours has a job. When every shade can be a background, headline or button, each design begins with the same decisions again.

Give the palette roles: one colour holds the structure, one leads attention, one marks the next step and a quiet neutral gives everything room. The combinations can change without making the brand feel newly invented each time.

Save this and label the job of every colour in your palette.
