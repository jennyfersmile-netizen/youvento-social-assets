# Your profile needs more than one clue

- Pillar: Profile clarity
- Format: Branded educate carousel, 5 slides, 1080 x 1350 px
- CTA: Save this
- Audience: Cold audience; small business owners whose profile still requires explanation
- Claims: General profile education only. No product, price, timing, inclusion or guarantee claims.
- Logo: Exact canonical `brand/youvento_logo_white.png`, preserved without cropping, distortion or redrawing.

## Slide copy

1. Private frustration: You rewrite the bio. The profile still makes sense only when you explain it yourself. Title: Your profile needs more than one clue.
2. One line cannot do every job. A visitor reads the whole profile, not only the bio.
3. Four useful clues: Name—what kind of work is this? Bio—what is being offered? Covers—what will I understand here? Pinned posts—where should I begin?
4. Agreement is not repetition. Weak: the same vague phrase everywhere. Clear: each surface answers a different part of the same question.
5. The useful test: Hide the bio for ten seconds. Do the name, covers and pinned posts still point to the same business? Save this and check every clue.

## Accessibility notes

- Large type and short lines for comfortable mobile reading.
- High-contrast white and pale-blue text on navy panels.
- Meaning is stated in text and does not rely on colour alone.
- Cover remains legible at grid-thumbnail size.
