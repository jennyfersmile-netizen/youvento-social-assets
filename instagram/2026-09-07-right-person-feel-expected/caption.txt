You can explain the offer carefully and still notice the people you hoped to reach moving past the profile without recognising themselves in it.

Clarity is not only about saying what the business does. It is also about making the right person feel that their situation was considered before they arrived. Name the moment they are in, use words they would naturally use and offer a next step that fits what they know so far.

You do not need to exclude everyone else or flatten people into a target-audience label. You need enough specificity for someone to think, quietly and quickly, “This was made with me in mind.”
