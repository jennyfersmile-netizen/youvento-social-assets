## Slide copy

1. Private frustration: You chose colours you love—then hesitate over which one belongs on the next post. Title: Your colour palette needs jobs, not just shades.
2. A palette without roles is just a list. It shows what can be used. It does not make the next choice easier.
3. Four useful roles: Base holds the structure. Lead carries the main idea. Signal marks action or emphasis. Quiet creates breathing room.
4. Illustrative example, not a client: Navy holds backgrounds. Blue leads headings. Pink marks the next step. Soft neutral makes space.
5. The useful test: Could someone choose the background, headline and CTA colours without asking you? Save this and label the job of every colour in your palette.
