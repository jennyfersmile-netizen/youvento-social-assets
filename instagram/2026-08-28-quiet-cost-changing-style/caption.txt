You can love this week’s look and still feel tempted to replace it before it has had time to become recognisable.

The quiet cost of changing your style every week is not only what a visitor sees. It is the time you spend choosing another palette, another font pairing and another layout before the next post can begin.

A visual style does not need to be permanent. It needs to stay long enough to do its job. Keep three rules steady for 30 days—how colour works, how headlines look and what a cover promises—then let the composition, imagery and pacing change around them.

Save this when a new mood makes you want to rebuild everything.
