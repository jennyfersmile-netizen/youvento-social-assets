You can have thoughtful testimonials ready and still feel the decision stall at the same point.

Proof only helps when it answers the doubt standing closest to the next step. A kind review may show that people enjoyed working with you, but someone who is unsure about fit, process or what happens after they enquire needs a more specific answer.

Place proof near the claim or decision it supports. Show the relevant detail, name what the example demonstrates and let it resolve one hesitation instead of asking a visitor to interpret a wall of praise.
