You can make a strong cover and still feel the carousel go flat the moment someone swipes.

That often happens when slide two repeats the promise in slightly different words. The reader has already understood enough to enter. Now they need a small reward for that decision: the hidden reason, the first useful distinction or one step that makes the subject clearer.

Think of the cover as the doorway and slide two as the first room. They should belong to the same place, but they should not show the same view.

Save this and read only the first two frames of your next carousel. Did the meaning move forward?
