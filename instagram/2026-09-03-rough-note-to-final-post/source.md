## Slide copy

1. You have the thought. It still does not feel like a post. What happens between your rough note and the final post.
2. Start with the tension, not the layout. What feels difficult now? What should feel clearer after reading?
3. Write the sentence the design must protect. One useful point. Plain enough to repeat. Specific enough to matter.
4. Give every slide one job. Hook, context, useful point, next step. If two jobs compete, split them.
5. Design the path, then test the real use. Cover at grid size. Text on a phone. Contrast, crop and logo. One fitting CTA.
6. A finished post is not a prettier note. It is a clearer decision. Save this for your next rough-note day.
