You can have the idea clearly in your head and still feel stuck when it is time to turn it into a post.

The missing piece is often not inspiration. It is a sequence of decisions. First, find the tension inside the rough note. Then choose the one thing the reader should understand, give each slide one job and let the design make that path easy to follow.

The final checks are quiet but important: can the cover be understood small, is the text comfortable on a phone and does the next step belong to the message? A finished post is not simply a prettier note. It is a clearer one.

Save this for the next idea that feels harder to finish than it should.
