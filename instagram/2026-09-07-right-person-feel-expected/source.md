## Slide copy

1. You explain what you do—and the right people still scroll past. Your profile should make the right person feel expected.
2. A visitor is not only asking, “What do you sell?” The question underneath is: “Is this meant for someone in my situation?”
3. Three signals: name the moment, use their words and size the next step.
4. Copy example, not a client: move from “Helping brands grow online” to “For small teams whose profile no longer matches their work.”
5. The right-person test: Could the wrong person read this and still think it is for them? Save this before your next profile edit.
