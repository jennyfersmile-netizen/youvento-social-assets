## Slide copy

1. You bold the headline, enlarge the number and brighten the button. Then the post gets harder to read. When everything is emphasised, nothing leads.
2. A focal point answers one quiet question: where should I look first? One idea arrives before the rest.
3. Give the eye an order: primary idea, supporting point, useful detail, next step. Hierarchy is sequence, not decoration.
4. Scale, contrast, space and position can all create attention. Choose one dominant signal. Let the others support it.
5. Shrink the design. Soften your focus. What arrives first? Is it the point the post is here to make? Save this before your next export.
