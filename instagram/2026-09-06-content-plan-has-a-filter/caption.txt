You can collect good ideas all week and still open the content calendar with no idea which one belongs there.

The problem is not a shortage of inspiration. It is the absence of a filter. Without one, every idea stays equally urgent—even when it speaks to the wrong person, repeats a job another post already does or leads somewhere the business is not trying to go.

Before adding an idea, check three things: relevance, role and route. If it fits all three, keep it. If the core matters but the angle does not, reshape it. If it belongs somewhere else, let it go without calling it a bad idea.

Save this before filling your next content calendar.
