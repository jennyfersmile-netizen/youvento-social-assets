## Slide copy

1. The first draft is allowed to look wrong.
2. A first draft has one useful job: make the hidden decisions visible.
3. Keep what carries the idea. Release what only fills the page.
4. Place, notice, remove, strengthen: a calm revision loop.
5. Do not ask the first draft to look finished. Ask what it taught you.
