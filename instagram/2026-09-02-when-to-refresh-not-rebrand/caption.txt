You can know the profile needs attention and still hesitate over how much of the brand to change.

Not every tired visual needs a new identity. Refresh when the offer, audience and promise still fit, but the way they appear no longer supports them. Rebrand when that foundation itself has changed—and the old identity is now telling the wrong story.

The useful question is not “How different can this look?” It is “What is still true, and what must remain recognisable?” Let that answer decide the size of the change.
