You can shorten the copy, make the type larger and still wonder why the sentence feels awkward on the slide.

Sometimes the problem is not the words. It is where the line breaks ask the reader to pause. A break after the wrong word can separate an idea from what completes it, bury the emphasis or make a natural sentence sound mechanical.

Read the line aloud before styling it. Keep small meaning-groups together, let the important phrase land where the eye can find it and avoid leaving a quiet word stranded on its own.
