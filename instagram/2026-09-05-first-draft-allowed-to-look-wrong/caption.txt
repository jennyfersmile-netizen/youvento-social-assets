You can know exactly what you want a post to feel like—and feel discouraged when the first version looks nothing like it.

That version is not failing. It is making invisible decisions visible. Once the words and shapes are on the page, you can finally see where the point gets buried, which part feels unlike the brand and what the idea no longer needs.

Revision is not punishment for getting it wrong. It is selection. Keep the sentence with energy, the order that guides the eye and the detail that feels true. Let the rest teach you what to change.

Save this for the next first draft that makes you doubt a good idea.
