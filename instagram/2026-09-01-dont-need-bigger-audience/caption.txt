You can be proud of the work and still feel as if the profile needs a larger audience before it deserves to look established.

Follower count and readiness are different things. A visitor cannot see the care behind the business unless the profile brings it forward: what you offer, who it is for, why the work is believable and where to begin.

Those are decisions you can make at any audience size. Let the profile become ready for the people who arrive now—not only for the number you hope to reach later.
