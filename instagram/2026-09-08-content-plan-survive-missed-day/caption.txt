You can miss one planned posting day and feel as if the whole month has slipped out of your hands.

A useful content plan leaves room for that. It gives each post a job and the month a direction, so the dates can move without turning the strategy into a pile of disconnected ideas.

You do not need to squeeze two posts into tomorrow or abandon the calendar. Move the next useful message forward, keep the sequence understandable and continue from where real life interrupted it.

Save this for the next week that refuses to follow the plan.
